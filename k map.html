Minterm Identification & Map Population
Examining the inputs and mapping all high outputs ($1$), low outputs ($0$), and don't-care states ($X$) to the corresponding Gray-coded cells.

Grouping by Gray Code Adjacencies & Powers of 2
According to Karnaugh Map theorems, adjacent cells differ by exactly one binary bit. Groups must form rectangular blocks of size $2^k$ (16, 8, 4, 2, or 1 cell) including edge-wrapping and 4-corner adjacencies.
3
